Tools

Four scripts that do what the methods notes describe · standard-library Python · 23 tests

Deletion ratePython Forty-seven lakh electors removed: alarming, or ordinary churn? Runs the July 2026 note's four checks, denominator, baseline attrition, draft or final, and who bears the error, on any roll figure. Poverty linesPython Is $2.15 a day more or less than the Tendulkar line? Puts the World Bank and Indian committee lines in rupees per person per month at one price year, using the World Bank's PPP and CPI series, and finds the price year behind any figure you are handed. Odds and likelihoodPython Is an odds ratio of 3 "three times as likely"? Converts odds and probability, computes odds ratio against relative risk from a 2x2 table and says when they diverge, and plots a binomial likelihood so it is visibly not a probability. AuditsPython Does each recommendation in the report rest on a finding, an assumption, evidence for it, and a way it could be wrong? Does "participatory" match where authority sits? Two checklists as JSON you fill in, and a check that fails the report until they are answered.

Each script names the edition it comes from. python -m pytest tests/ runs the suite; nothing needs installing beyond pytest. Full guide in tools/.

How it stays current

Scripts, not hands

scripts/sync_substack.py reads the public Substack API, writes each post to archive/ as Markdown with front matter, and rebuilds the archive index and the recurring-sections index. scripts/build_companions.py reads the archive and rebuilds the eight companion pages. A monthly workflow runs both and opens a pull request when anything changed; nothing lands on main without the tests and lints passing. build_companions.py --check fails CI if the companions drift from the archive.

Part of the OpenStacks family: InsightStack for MEL tools and survey data loaders, FieldStack for field operations in R, EquityStack for distributional analysis in Python. SignalStack is the newsletter's companion, beside them rather than one of them.

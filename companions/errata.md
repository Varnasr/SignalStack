## May 2026, Research praxis: the Tendulkar line's price year

The edition says the Tendulkar rural line "sits at roughly Rs 1,622 per
person per month ... at 2011-12 prices".

At 2011-12 prices the line is Rs 816 (Planning Commission press note, 22 July
2013; urban Rs 1,000). Rs 816 inflated to 2023 prices by the World Bank CPI
series for India (108.91 in 2011, 216.86 in 2023) is Rs 1,625. The figure is
the 2023 value with the wrong year attached.

`python tools/poverty_lines.py --check 1622 --line tendulkar_rural` shows
the calculation.

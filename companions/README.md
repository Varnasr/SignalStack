# Companions

Each page gathers one recurring section of the newsletter from every edition
it appeared in, newest first, with a link to the edition. The text is the
newsletter's.

| Page | Section | Entries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Research praxis](praxis.md) | One note per edition on reading, writing or judging evidence | 7 |
| [Disambiguation corner](glossary.md) | Terms that are commonly confused | 10 |
| [Methods corner](methods.md) | Short methods notes and research history | 4 |
| [Datasets worth opening](datasets.md) | Named datasets and what to check before using them | 1 |
| [Researchers worth following](researchers.md) | One entry per person, by surname | 15 |
| [Papers and reports](reading.md) | Recommended reading, by edition | 35 |
| [Books and long reads](books.md) | Books, a documentary and long reads, by edition | 13 |
| [Tools and free resources](tools.md) | Platforms and resources, by edition | 11 |

[Errata](errata.md) lists corrections to the newsletter's text. The eight
pages above are generated by `scripts/build_companions.py`; edit the
script, not the pages.

Four of the notes have a script in [`tools/`](../tools/).

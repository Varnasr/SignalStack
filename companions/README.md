# Companions

The parts of an edition that outlive it. Each page pulls one recurring
section of the newsletter out of every edition it appeared in and lays the
entries end to end, newest first, with a link back to the edition. The text
is the newsletter's; nothing is rewritten.

| Page | What it compiles | Entries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Research praxis](praxis.md) | One note per edition on how to read, write or judge evidence | 7 |
| [Disambiguation corner](glossary.md) | Terms that get swapped for each other, and why the difference changes a number | 10 |
| [Methods corner](methods.md) | Short methods notes and research history | 4 |
| [Datasets worth opening](datasets.md) | Named datasets and what to read before using them | 1 |
| [Researchers worth following](researchers.md) | One entry per person, alphabetical by surname | 15 |
| [Papers and reports](reading.md) | Everything recommended reading, by edition | 35 |
| [Books and long reads](books.md) | Books, a documentary and long reads, by edition | 13 |
| [Tools and free resources](tools.md) | Platforms and resources, by edition | 11 |

[Errata](errata.md) lists the places the newsletter's own text is known to
be wrong. It is hand-written; the eight pages above are generated by
`scripts/build_companions.py` and overwritten on every run.

Four of the praxis and methods notes have a script in
[`tools/`](../tools/): the deletion-rate checks, the poverty-line
conversion, odds against likelihood, and the recommendation and
participation audits.
